# Supported capture and limits

Use paper, photos or thin rectangular cards on a flat table. The reference
black square and measured surface must share that plane. Thick books, boxes,
curves, height and different planes are unsupported. The app estimates two
side dimensions; it does not recognize arbitrary objects or reconstruct 3D.

Use the main phone camera, avoid ultra-wide mode, and keep the marker close
to the target. Lens distortion is not corrected. Image edges, tiny markers,
blur, glare, occlusion and extrapolation far from the reference increase error.
Strong perspective can make corner placement unreliable even when a numerical
transform exists. Manual corrections remain essential.

Print the marker at actual size with no fit-to-page. Measure the outer black
square with a ruler and enter that actual measurement. Quiet-zone width is
not part of the reference size. The suggested 50 mm is never ground truth.

Synthetic tests establish numerical consistency and detector smoke behavior.
They do not establish real-photo accuracy. Real-photo performance and the
provisional 5%/one-second goals await collected photographs, confirmed corners,
ruler measurements and documented hardware. Numerical portfolio claims must
come from that evaluation.
